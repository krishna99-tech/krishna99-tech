### 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm a **software & embedded systems developer** who loves building things that live at the intersection of hardware and cloud. My work spans **real-time IIoT ecosystems**, **serverless architectures**, **microservices & APIs**, and **cross-platform mobile/web applications**.

* ⚡ **Hardware & IoT:** Developing robust firmware for **edge devices & MCUs** using **FreeRTOS**, low-power **BLE provisioning**, and **mTLS encrypted MQTT**.
* ☁️ **Cloud & APIs:** Building high-speed APIs and serverless microservices with **FastAPI**, **Flask**, **Node.js**, and **AWS Lambda**.
* 📱 **Frontend & Mobile:** Crafting modern web and mobile experiences with **Next.js**, **React**, and **Flutter**.
* 🐳 **DevOps & Containers:** Packaging scalable services with **Docker** and automated CI/CD workflows.
* 🤖 **AI-Augmented Engineering:** Leveraging AI tools to accelerate prototyping, test architecture, and ship production-ready code.
